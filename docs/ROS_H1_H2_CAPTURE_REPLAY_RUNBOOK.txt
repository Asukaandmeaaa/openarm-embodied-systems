5. Export recorded commands AND measured motion (offline)
=========================================================
  source /home/lushilin/openarm_vla_ws/scripts/home_replay/workstation_env.sh
  export CAP=$(cat "$VLA_CAPTURE_ROOT/results/active_h1h2_capture.path")
  cd "$VLA_CAPTURE_ROOT"
  .venv-real-act/bin/python scripts/home_replay/export.py "$CAP" \
    --phase H2 --source command --output "$CAP/H2_command_replay.json"
  .venv-real-act/bin/python scripts/home_replay/export.py "$CAP" \
    --phase H2 --source measured --output "$CAP/H2_measured_replay.json"

command: decoded MIT position references plus recorded dq/kp/kd/torque; an archive
of controller commands, not automatically an admissible geometric replay path.
measured: the actual measured geometric path, used for right-only simulation.
Export preserves command/feedback start offsets in start_alignment. A successful
export does not imply a successful replay-entry check or physical authorization.

Both contain all 16 named axes and timestamps; all selected source knots are
retained. Per-axis timestamp alignment prevents artificial microsteps caused by
CAN frames arriving sequentially. Piecewise linear interpolation connects only
adjacent recorded samples; it does not generate a zero/side/HOME shortcut.

Export fails on missing initial axes, >100 ms data gaps, dropped/error CAN frames,
clock discontinuities, non-enabled feedback, lifecycle operations in the interval,
unstable final pose, ROS/CAN final mismatch, stale H2 report, config/hash mismatch,
or absent full accepted plans. Never bypass a failed export by trimming the prefix.

H1 startup is retained in raw CAN/ROS evidence, including enable/disable operations
and the initial zero command. A whole H1 activation interval will normally NOT
be exportable as a position-only trajectory: it has lifecycle operations, and
both arms may not have complete feedback before the first movement. That is an
explicit refusal, not silent truncation. H2 replay requires its already-recorded
near-zero starting state; physical motor activation must be handled separately.

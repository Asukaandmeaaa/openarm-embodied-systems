Control arrangement
===================
ROS ros2_control remains the only intended CAN writer. Its existing gains, gravity
compensation, joint limits, controller update rate and interpolation settings are
reused. MoveIt sends controller actions to /authorized/... at a resident gateway.
The gateway forwards them only while an H2 lease exists. Dora uses a mutually
exclusive Unix-socket lease; its DDS identity cannot write controller topics or
call controller actions. Left arm and gripper are not Dora action targets.

Why holding uses replacement goals rather than ordinary action cancellation
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The reviewed JTC 4.40.1 cancellation callback uses set_hold_position() when
deceleration-on-cancel is not enabled. That function takes measured positions,
not the existing commanded positions. A tracking offset can contribute holding
torque through the position controller; discarding it can change support torque.
The gateway instead preempts the old action with a zero-time, position-only
hold action at the existing controller OUTPUT (or the last accepted Dora target
when releasing Dora). It requires successful hold results, matching output
references, fresh feedback and settling before admitting another source.
This is already implemented; do not deliberately reproduce the old cancellation
behavior on hardware. The 0.02 rad offset mock test verified reference preservation.
It does not prove real torque continuity or certify an emergency stop. Controller
internal fault/abort paths still exist; this is not a global patch to ROS JTC.

DDS Security uses default-DENY signed permissions and rejects unauthenticated
participants. Ordinary legacy ROS, RViz, teleop and direct trajectory/action
clients cannot bypass the gateway through raw ROS endpoints. Passive-mode and
controller-manager mutation requests are not granted to H2, MoveIt or Dora.

One intentional startup change: the gripper spawner loads ONLY the right
gripper controller. The known left-gripper measured-limit discrepancy previously
could abort the combined spawner before the right controller was loaded. The
left arm controller remains active, and ROS hardware keeps the left gripper
initial holding reference. No left limit or gain is relaxed; no left gripper
trajectory controller is admitted during this right-arm commissioning session.

The startup spawners have a separate trusted bootstrap identity, permitted to
load/configure/activate controllers. Wait until ALL spawners exit before claiming
motion authority. Do not use bootstrap credentials for interactive commands.
The keystore is private to the workstation user. This protects configured
program identities, not hostile same-user software stealing their private keys,
root, or arbitrary direct-CAN tools. The ROS/Dora CAN lock is cooperative and
covers the patched drivers; it is not a Linux firewall against CAN transmitters.

controller goals, because stock JTC publishes no initial empty action-status
message. Wait until status reports idle/ready and the current hold is verified.
Do not acquire a lease while synchronizing, draining, faulted, or running goals.

Expected ready status: status.state="idle", synchronized=true, drain_ready=true,
pending=[] and hold_pending=[], with every raw_active list empty. The required
right arm, right gripper and left arm controllers must all report "active".
Initial idle with synchronized=false is NOT readiness. Repeat only the status
command while synchronization finishes; do not repeatedly issue synchronize-hold.

6. Ending the manual validation
==============================
For an ordinary interruption during H2 or hold-test, press Ctrl-C in the COMMAND
PRODUCER terminal, leaving H1 and the gateway running. The client disconnect
revokes its lease; the gateway attempts replacement holds. In another terminal:

  /usr/bin/python3 -m scripts.control_ownership.client --socket "$OPENARM_AUTHORITY_SOCKET" status

Require no pending/active goals, verified holds and actual physical settling.
Cancellation can leave a latched fault: preserve logs and investigate rather than
automatically resetting or rerunning from the interrupted pose. A software hold
is not an instantaneous brake and is not a substitute for the physical E-stop.

For unexpected movement toward an obstacle, sag, uncontrolled oscillation or
loss of trustworthy control, use the physical emergency stop immediately; do not
wait for a CLI response. Torque may be lost: keep people out of the motion path,
and support the arms only when safe. Do not reach into a moving arm to catch it.
After an emergency stop, re-evaluate the pose and mechanical-zero startup before
any re-enable; do not simply rerun H1 from an arbitrary interrupted pose.

After the client reports idle, stop only the gateway if desired; leave ROS H1
running to hold both arms. Graceful gateway stop revokes admission and attempts
acknowledged holds; it never disables motors. An abrupt gateway crash/SIGKILL
cannot perform cleanup: ROS retains its accepted target or may finish a timed
H2 goal already accepted. Restarting the gateway always requires a NEW explicit
synchronize-hold before any source can acquire authority. Never remove the
socket while a live gateway owns its lock; after a confirmed dead process, inspect
and archive the stale socket path before clearing it as a manual recovery step.

To shut down ROS H1 itself, first physically support BOTH arms and follow the
existing power-down procedure. Ctrl-C of the hardware launch disables motors;
it is not a torque-preserving source switch.

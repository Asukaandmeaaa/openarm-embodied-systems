Trajectory / Action Interface - Frozen Decision
================================================

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: FROZEN after G5.5.2 controlled replay
Authority: official OpenArm executor source plus fixed-source counterfactual replay

Decision
--------

Dataset and policy contract:

  30 Hz observation.state + camera observations
  30 Hz absolute joint-position action labels

Deployment contract:

  ACT 30 Hz position waypoints
    -> official HermiteUpsampler (250 Hz target grid)
    -> optional official BiquadLowpass (15 Hz)
    -> direct MuJoCo / robot position target

The dataset action is the safe target that was actually submitted during expert
collection.  Do not precompute 250 Hz Hermite/Biquad outputs as ACT labels and do
not synthesize a 60 Hz training dataset from the 30 Hz demonstrations.

Evidence
--------

1. The same raw 30 Hz expert trajectory succeeds at every tested command
   cadence (30, 40, 50, 60, 90, and 120 Hz) when the official interpolation
   result is sent directly to position control.  There is no observed 60 Hz
   minimum-cadence requirement.

2. The 30 Hz, 3.3 s ACT-style chunk path succeeds for all five pilot episodes
   with official Hermite + 15 Hz Biquad + direct control when a common 2 s
   terminal hold is included.

3. Reapplying the project ActionGuard to already-safe expert targets reduces
   the five-episode result from 5/5 to 2/5.  The full cadence sweep is 12/12 for
   direct control and 0/12 for the guarded counterfactual (seed 20260902).

4. The old converter default applied a 1 Hz fourth-order filter while assuming
   a 250 Hz input rate, even though the source is 30 Hz.  It altered labels by
   up to 0.12155 rad and the converted trajectory failed strict replay.  The
   no-smoothing conversion is pointwise identical to the raw sampler and passes
   all five official-chain replays.

Component responsibilities
--------------------------

HermiteUpsampler
  Temporal interpolation from policy knots to the executor clock.  It does not
  restore information absent from the 30 Hz demonstrations.

BiquadLowpass
  Optional deployment filter from the official executor.  It is compatible
  with the expert trajectory, but its delay requires executing the final target
  long enough to drain the filter.  It is not part of the training-label
  construction.

Position controller / data.ctrl
  Consumes the interpolated (and optionally filtered) position target directly,
  matching the official dora-openarm-mujoco path.

Safety boundary
---------------

Retain checks for shape, finite values, actuator ctrlrange, freshness/watchdog,
and emergency stop.  The current ActionGuard velocity/acceleration trajectory
generator must not be placed after the official executor for these expert/ACT
targets: that is a second trajectory parameterization and is not semantically
transparent.  If a dynamic safety filter is required for hardware, design and
validate it as a separate control contract against task-level replay; do not
assume that low clamp counts prove equivalence.

Training implications
---------------------

* Use data/lerobot/openarm_cube_pick_expert_pilot_v3_nosmoothing.
* Keep dataset and ACT action cadence at 30 Hz.
* Train on the unmodified 30 Hz action labels.
* Interpret a 1000-step ACT run as a smoke test, not a trained task policy.
* Require a one-episode memorization gate and then a multi-episode closed-loop
  gate before expanding data or starting another policy family.
